## Other 3D Printing Technologies

### Overview
- **Course:** Engineering Interaction Technologies
- **Professor:** Michael Wessely, Interactive Matter Lab

### Diverse 3D Printing Methods

#### Fused Deposition Modeling (FDM)

![Desktop View](https://linkingblog.oss-eu-central-1.aliyuncs.com/picgo/20241227021234.png){: width="872" height="489" .w-50 .right}

- **Description:** Plastic filament is extruded through a hot nozzle, melting and then solidifying to form layers.
- **Equipment:** Original Prusa i3 MK3S kit, among others available at IDC.

#### Direct Ink Writing (DIW)

![Desktop View](https://linkingblog.oss-eu-central-1.aliyuncs.com/picgo/20241227021430.png){: width="872" height="489" .w-50 .right}

- **Process:** Similar to FDM but uses cold extrusion to print pastes, such as food or ceramics, typically using a syringe and air pressure(cold extrusion).
- **Applications:** Food fabrication, ceramic printing.

#### Laminated Object Manufacturing (LOM)

![Desktop View](https://linkingblog.oss-eu-central-1.aliyuncs.com/picgo/20241227022024.png){: width="872" height="489" .w-50 .right}

- **Technique:** Layers of paper or other materials are colored, glued, and cut into shape to form an object.(first sheet **2D color printed**, then glued, then cut into shape)
- **Materials:** Can include conductive traces for heating, allowing integration with electronic functions.

#### Stereolithography (SLA) and Digital Light Processing (DLP)



- **SLA:** Uses a laser to cure liquid resin in a tank, building layers as the platform lowers. (使用激光在液态树脂中逐点固化，逐层构建物体。)
![Desktop View](https://linkingblog.oss-eu-central-1.aliyuncs.com/picgo/20241227022211.png)
- **DLP:** Similar to SLA but uses a projector, allowing entire layers to solidify at once, enhancing speed but affecting resolution due to pixel shape.(使用投影仪一次性固化整个层面，提高速度。)
![Desktop View](https://linkingblog.oss-eu-central-1.aliyuncs.com/picgo/20241227022228.png)

- what are some benets / drawbacks of using a projector vs laser?
  - using a projector's benefit:
    - faster
    - resolution and surface finish due to sqaure pixels
  - using a projector's drawback:
    - material is relatively expensive
    - complicated following steps, needs to be clean and solidify
  
#### Selective Laser Sintering (SLS)
![Desktop View](https://linkingblog.oss-eu-central-1.aliyuncs.com/picgo/20241227023336.png)
- **Mechanism:** A laser fuses powder materials layer by layer, suitable for metals, glass, and ceramics.(使用喷墨头将粘合剂喷洒在粉末床上，逐层粘结成型。)
- **Note:** Primarily industrial due to the high temperatures involved.
  - benefits in SLS
    - suitable for complicated geometric shape without an addtional supportive structure
    - high toughness, fits in functional component
  - drawbacks in SLS
    - expensive device, industry
    - following steps are complicated

#### Binder Jetting (BJ)
![Desktop View](https://linkingblog.oss-eu-central-1.aliyuncs.com/picgo/20241227023443.png)
- **Process:** Uses inkjet heads to deposit binder onto a powder bed, layer by layer.(类似于2D喷墨打印，但在三维空间中喷射并用紫外光固化)
- **Capabilities:** Can produce full-color objects by printing onto powder.

#### Polyjetting
- **Method:** Similar to 2D inkjet printing but on three-dimensional objects, curing the deposited ink with UV light.

### Innovations and Advanced Applications
- **Experimental Technologies:**
  - **Organovo:** Focuses on bioprinting, particularly human organs.
  - **Carbon 3D:** Known for significantly faster printing processes.
  - **ExpandFab, LaserFactory, PrintFlatables:** Explore new materials and fabrication methods that extend the capabilities of traditional 3D printing.

### Summary
- **Future Directions:** Encourages the creation of custom printing processes and materials, showcasing the expansive potential of 3D printing technology in various fields, from medical applications to consumer electronics.
