01Overview

Two or three sentences a busy reader could stop at and still understand the work: the problem, what you made, and the headline result.

Keep this paragraph free of jargon. Everything below can go deeper.

02Background & motivation

What is broken today, and why existing approaches do not solve it. Cite prior work sparingly and in plain language.

End with the specific research question this project answers.

03Method & system

Describe the artifact: architecture, interaction model, key design decisions and the trade-offs behind them.

If there is a novel technique, give it one short paragraph and one figure rather than a wall of text.

04Study & evaluation

Participants, task, conditions, measures. One paragraph is usually enough at portfolio depth.

Say why this design was the right way to test the claim.

05Results

Lead with the finding, then the evidence. Quantitative effects with direction and magnitude; qualitative themes with one short quote each.

Close with limitations and what you would do next — reviewers read this as maturity.
