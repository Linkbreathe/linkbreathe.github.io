Distilling GPT-4 Preferences for Summarization

Metric calibration and Direct Preference Optimization for more factual, concise, and preference-aligned Reddit TL;DR summaries under limited compute.

Metric calibration / DPO

From proxy scores to better summaries.

Flan-T5 · ROUGE-L · BERTScore · NLI factuality · GPT-4 judgments

01Question

Supervised summarization models can produce fluent text while still missing what people value: factual accuracy, relevance, and concision. This project asks whether a small preference dataset, built from calibrated automatic metrics, can move a Flan-T5 summarizer toward better Reddit TL;DRs without a full reward-modeling or reinforcement-learning pipeline.

The central design tension is that no single metric captures summary quality. Lexical overlap, semantic similarity, and factual consistency each reward a different aspect of the output.

02Pipeline

The system first fine-tunes a Flan-T5 baseline on a cleaned Reddit TL;DR corpus. Multiple encoder-decoder models and decoding settings then generate diverse candidate summaries for the same posts.

03Metric calibration

We compare 400 candidate pairs with GPT-4 judgments and search over the simplex of metric weights. The best combination puts most weight on BERTScore while retaining explicit factuality and a small lexical-overlap contribution.

0.020ROUGE-L
0.780BERTScore
0.200Factuality

The calibrated score filters pairs using a factuality threshold, a minimum score gap, and a minimum quality requirement for the human-written TL;DR. This yields 19,796 preference pairs: 6,569 human-over-model comparisons and 13,227 model-over-model comparisons.

04What changed

Three DPO models vary the regularization coefficient β while keeping the training setup fixed. On 500 held-out test posts, all DPO variants improve the NLI-based factuality score and slightly improve BERTScore, while ROUGE-L decreases as the model rewrites more aggressively.

+0.0943Factuality gain · β 0.01
0.7912Best BERTScore · β 0.1
52.0%GPT-4 win rate · β 0.1

The result is not a simple metric win. The most aggressive factuality improvement produces the weakest GPT-4 preference score, while moderate β values offer the better balance between stability and preference alignment.

05Reflection

This project made the limits of proxy rewards concrete. An NLI-based factuality signal can detect overt contradictions while missing plausible hallucinations or stylistic drift. GPT-4 judgments expose that gap by evaluating factuality, coverage, concision, and naturalness together.

The broader lesson is that preference alignment needs evaluation signals that remain expressive after they are operationalised. Calibration helps, but it does not remove the need for richer preference data and careful qualitative inspection.
